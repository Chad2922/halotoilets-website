Request Location Removal

Business owners and facility managers can request removal of a listing from the Halo Toilets app and database.

Halo Toilets aggregates publicly accessible washroom data from open government datasets, OpenStreetMap, and community contributions. We respect the rights of business owners and will review all removal requests. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

Removal Request Form

What Happens Next

  • We will review your request within 5 business days
  • We may follow up to verify ownership or authorization
  • If approved, the listing will be removed from the app and our database
  • You will receive confirmation by email once the removal is complete

Prefer to Email Directly?

Send your removal request directly to us — please include the business name, full address, and your relationship to the business.
